The metric examples are starting points from the Lab 3 metric list. Choose your own.

## Your panel, step by step

1. Comment "started" on your issue.
2. Pick **3 or 4 metrics** for your panel.
3. For each metric, write one short line on each of:
   - **Definition:** what it counts, and how it is calculated.
   - **Target or alert level:** for example "false positives under 1%", the case target.
   - **Decision:** what management would do if it moves.
4. Sketch how your panel would look on the dashboard: a number tile, a trend line, a bar chart or a table. A hand sketch or a simple shape in Google Slides is enough.
5. Write it in your own words, from your own Assignment 1 work. Keep it short: a poster, not a report.
6. Put your panel in the poster file (link in Google Chat) by **Wed Dec 2**, or send it to Mehul.
7. Comment "done" on your issue.

**Mehul:** sets up the poster file and layout, fills in the product panel, joins the four panels on **Thu Dec 3**, checks it, and submits it to Canvas by **Fri Dec 4**.
