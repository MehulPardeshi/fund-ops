## What we hand in and when

| Part | What | Who | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Funding pitch | 1 to 3 minute pitch of Veritas: what it does, who it is for, the problem it solves, challenges (technical, market and economic, security, privacy, safety, governance) and **how much funding we ask for** | Team | In class, **Tue Dec 1** |
| Pitch slide deck | One shared deck | Team | Submitted |
| Pitch video | Pre-recorded video of the pitch | Team | Submitted |
| Investor feedback | Rate the other teams' pitches and how much you would invest, in a Google Form | Each person | In class, Tue Dec 1 |
| Merger or acquisition decision | Merge with, acquire, or stay independent from the other company working on the same problem. One slide and a 1 minute presentation | Team | In class, **Thu Dec 3** |
| Decision form | Merger and acquisition Google Form response | Team | Thu Dec 3 |
| Peer review | Agree or disagree with each other company's decision, in a Google Form | Each person | In class, Thu Dec 3 |
| Metrics Challenge | "The first part or requirement of the final project." Uses the Metrics Challenge code from the professor's email. After finishing the AI Red Team challenges (Assignment 2), email the teaching team to receive the challenge dataset, which is needed for the final project submission | To be confirmed (asked the professor whether it is per person or per team) | Not posted |

Grades come from the substance of our Assignment 1 and 2 work, the presentation and participation. Being open about our product's limitations and risks counts. Asking for more money does not earn a better grade, and you must not offer other teams less funding than you think they deserve.

## How we split the pitch

Four blocks, one per person. Each block owns its slides (1 to 2) and records its own 30 to 45 second part of the video. Mehul assembles the deck and joins the clips. The pitch is 1 to 3 minutes in total, so keep each part short.

| Block | Content | Reuse from Assignment 1 | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| product | Product, problem, users; title and closing slide; assembles the deck and the video | Module A (workflow, display, student messages) | Mehul |
| market | Market and competitors, pricing and unit economics, **the funding ask** (amount and what it pays for) | Module B (B1 to B4) | claim it |
| tech | How it works: architecture, models, data, infrastructure, capacity and cost | Modules C, D, E1 to E3 | claim it |
| risk | Risks and limitations: security, privacy, safety, governance, fairness, and how we manage them (also feeds everyone's Assignment 2 risk plan) | E4, D3, red-team findings | claim it |

Claim a block by commenting on the final project planning issue, or ask your AI assistant to claim it for you (it follows [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md)).
