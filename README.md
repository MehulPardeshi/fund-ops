# Fund Ops: Company B1 (Final Project Team 2)

Project tracker for 94-879 B2 *Fundamentals of Operationalizing AI*. We are Veritas AI Education, building an AI academic-integrity platform for Apex State University.

This repo is the **management layer only**. The work and the submissions live in Google:

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Notebook, model index spreadsheet, our individual folders | Company Drive folder `company_b_1` (Andrew account only) |
| Team decisions and the Division of Labor table | Company Google Chat (graded) |
| Self-evaluation form, completion email | Google Form, email |

## Start here

1. **Using Claude, ChatGPT or Codex?** Open this repo with it. It follows [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md): it shows the free blocks, claims one for you and walks you through your tasks.
2. **No AI?** Read [docs/PROJECT_GUIDE.md](docs/PROJECT_GUIDE.md), then run `python3 scripts/blocks.py`.
3. **Finishing up?** Follow [docs/SUBMIT.md](docs/SUBMIT.md).

## Rules

- **No names, emails or student IDs** in the notebook. Team number 2 is the only identifier.
- **Your answers must show your own reasoning.** The professor allows GenAI use as long as the reasoning is yours; answers with no personal reasoning lose marks.
- **Write your own block in your own words**, and do not copy a teammate's text.
- **No collaboration outside the team.**
- Do not commit course PDFs, the notebook, notebook outputs or exported data (see `.gitignore`). This repo is public.
